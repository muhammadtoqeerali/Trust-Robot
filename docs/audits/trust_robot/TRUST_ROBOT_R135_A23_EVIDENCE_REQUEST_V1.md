## Critical provenance distinction

A challenge identifier or SHA-256 digest can bind
a software artifact but cannot independently prove
a physical sensor capture.

A witness must be independent of the capture writer
within the applicable trust boundary.

A syntactically complete evidence packet does not
qualify physical timing, physical sensor calibration
or localization ground truth.
