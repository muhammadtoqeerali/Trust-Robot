# TRUST-ROBOT R135-A27-5 / A30-1 Readiness Audit

## Scope

This phase inspects checksum-pinned installed Nova Carter
calibration and sensor-launch sources and implements an
offline physical-measurement readiness review.

The inspection is read-only and does not launch ROS nodes,
record new sensor measurements or move the robot.

## A27-5 frame provenance

The inspected calibration contains 12 sensor records.
The nominal URDF contains 37 links and 36 joints.

The physical IMU publishes `bmi088_frame`, which is not
declared in the inspected nominal URDF.

Installed launch-file contents are useful for tracing
configuration and frame naming. They do not independently
establish a metric IMU-to-platform transform.

No frame equivalence, extrinsic uncertainty, physical
event-time definition or clock accuracy is qualified.

## A30-1 evidence crosswalk

The readiness validator reuses the exact frozen A23
prospective evidence requests:

- Stage A: A01 and A02, independent acquisition provenance.
- Stage B: B01 through B06, calibration and physical timing.
- Stage C: C01 through C03, independent six-DoF reference.
- Stage D: D01 and D02, prospective evaluation readiness.

All thirteen requests remain unfulfilled.

A checksum-verified ROS bag or internally consistent
calibration document is not a substitute for an independent
witness, applicable calibration uncertainty, or an
independent reference trajectory.

The new Nova Carter evidence does not retroactively
qualify historical M2DGR sequences.

7 validation-calibration and 7 confirmation-test sequences.

selection or fitting; historical confirmation inspection
means prospective pristine status cannot be claimed.
